**Cheeky Foveated DLSS v0.5.4**

This update improves eye-tracking stability during blinks, fixes a D3D12 VR compatibility issue, and strengthens DX11 transport and DLSS-NR stability since v0.5.3.

- **Gaze hold on signal loss:** a new advanced eye-tracking setting holds the last gaze position through brief tracking interruptions without resetting DLSS history. The default is 400 ms, adjustable from 0 to 1000 ms; longer interruptions still return smoothly to fixed placement.
- **D3D12 VR compatibility:** fixes a deadlock that could freeze Cyberpunk 2077 when entering R.E.A.L. VR with OFXR Bridge by keeping unrelated D3D12 command lists out of Cheeky's calibration synchronization.
- **Stable DLSS-NR history:** moving the foveated region through DX11 transport now preserves NR guide allocations and temporal history, avoiding unnecessary resets as gaze moves.
- **Safer DX11 transport cleanup:** waits for private DX12 GPU work to finish before releasing transport resources, including during shutdown and error recovery.

**Downloads:** Standalone and UEVR ZIPs only. Both include the matching OpenXR installer.

**Updating:** Exit the game before replacing files.

- **Standalone:** copy `CheekyFoveatedDLSS/` and **one** loader, `dxgi.dll` or `version.dll`, into the game's executable folder. Do not copy both loaders or overwrite another mod's loader. For Vulkan, also update `CheekyVulkanLayer.dll` and preserve `CheekyFoveatedDLSS/Vulkan/`. Open settings with F8 or your configured menu key.
- **UEVR:** extract the complete UEVR package into the game's UEVR configuration directory, preserving `plugins/` and `scripts/`. Update both DLLs and the Lua script together. Saved settings can stay in place.
- **OpenXR:** run the included `OpenXR/CheekyOpenXRSetup.exe` to update the shared layer alongside Cheeky, including on headsets without eye tracking.

DLSS-NR still requires a compatible `nvngx_dlssnr.dll`, supplied separately or available through a compatible NVIDIA App runtime override.
